The best movies according to Sarah!

My top 3 movies of all time.

1. Avengers: Endgame

The name alone says enough, the Endgame. If you're looking for a movie that keeps you on the edge of your seat the entire time, than no need to look any further. As a huge Marvel fan, this film was like the tip of the iceberg, the cherry on top of the cake. It's the first movie that comes to mind whenever someone asks me what my favorite movie is.

2. Tenet

Such a mindf*cking movie, but in the best way possible. In all honesty I still haven't fully figured the lore out, which makes me love it even more

3. The Princess switch

This movie made it to the list simply because it is nostalgic to me. Every December I would sleep over at my grandma's and it would be this movie that we watched. The acting isn't the best but think of it as a vibey Christmas movie. Now I dont have sleep overs anymore but yes I still watch the movie every year.
